### Occurrence creation and due access

Approved event-triggered immediate-due policy:

1. After a victory/failure result is durably settled, bind that eligible Attempt to one occurrence for the Pilot and this Learning Entry.
2. Reuse that Pilot/Entry's unfinished occurrence if it exists; otherwise create one with a stable identity and persisted `dueAt` equal to its creation WallClock time.
3. Commit creation/reuse and the Attempt-to-occurrence link atomically. Every result retains its link.
4. Reopening a linked result retrieves the linked occurrence, including when completed. It never creates another reward opportunity for that result.
5. Skip/leave/reload preserves the unfinished occurrence. Build exposes it as due without resetting first-response or hint evidence.
6. A later previously unlinked eligible result may create the next occurrence only after the earlier occurrence is complete. Multiple results while one is unfinished share it rather than resetting it.
7. Abandonment/interruption creates no occurrence or automatic offer. Existing Pilot due Review remains reachable from safe Build.
8. The occurrence completion, evidence, receipt and Credits publish atomically with stable operation identity across Retry.

No daily cap, recurrence interval, catch-up reward queue, SRS algorithm or delayed-recall threshold is selected by this fixture. It proves immediate due access from Build. Wider delayed-practice scheduling remains separate MVP work and must not be reported as delivered by Ticket10.
